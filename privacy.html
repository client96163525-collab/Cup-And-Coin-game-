Privacy Policy Active

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

Welcome to Cup and Coin (Cup and Coin 3D / Cup Shuffle 3D). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict data privacy policies.

1. Data Collection & Privacy

Cup and Coin 3D is a 100% offline-first application.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, phone number, location, or device ID.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: The game does not contain any third-party SDKs, tracking pixels, or user behavior analytics software.
  • No Ad Networks: We do not show intrusive ads and do not share any identifiers with ad partners.

2. Local Storage & Progress Saving

All your game progression data (including high scores, levels, completed challenges, and unlocked themes) is stored locally on your own device using Android's standard secure preferences database (SharedPreferences). This data remains strictly on your device and is never sent to any server.

3. Children's Privacy

Since we collect absolutely zero user data, our game is 100% compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) guidelines for all age groups, including kids.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect any personal data, any changes will be reflected by updating this page with the new effective date.
